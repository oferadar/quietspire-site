SLAB

A stacking game built on sliding and slicing. Tap to drop the slab. Land it short and the overhang is sliced off, so the next one is smaller.

Calm on the surface, with a real skill ceiling: it gets harder because your target shrinks, not because it speeds up. Hit the middle for a perfect. Chain a few and the slab grows back.

Free on iPhone. No wifi or account needed.

Get it on the App Store

What else is in SLAB

  • Crystals

    Skins and worlds to unlock with in-game crystals.

  • Every day

    A daily gift and three daily challenges.

  • As you climb

    The colors change the higher the stack goes.

  • Ads

    Some runs show a short ad, never before your first run. Rewarded ads are your choice, to revive a run or top up crystals. Remove Ads is an in-app purchase.

  • Your progress

    Your high score and crystals are saved on your device. Support and privacy have the details.
