Common questions

How do I add the widgets?
On iPhone: open Hapax once after installing (this hands the schedule to your widgets). Then: for the Home Screen, long-press an empty spot → Edit → Add Widget → Hapax. For the Lock Screen, long-press the Lock Screen → Customize → tap the area below the clock → Hapax. There are two Lock Screen styles: Word of the Day, with the definition, and Just the Word, the word alone as large as it fits.

On Android: touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Widgets, then find Hapax and drag it where you want it. On many phones Hapax can place it for you with one tap. To see the word on your lock screen, turn on "Word on your lock screen" in the app. It sets your lock screen wallpaper only; your home wallpaper is never changed.

When does the word change?
At midnight, your local time, on the widget itself: no need to open the app, and it works fully offline.

What does the Difficulty setting do?
It sets which level today's word and all future words come from: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced or Expert. Your past history keeps the words you actually saw: changing difficulty never rewrites it.

The pronunciation didn't play.
Tap the dotted-underlined respelling (for example, "HAY-paks") to hear the word. It uses your phone's built-in voice and needs no internet. On iPhone it plays even with the ringer on silent, so if you hear nothing, turn up the volume.

How do I restore a purchase?
Purchases are made through the App Store and belong to your Apple Account, so they carry over to a new phone or a reinstall. Hapax checks for them each time it opens. If something is missing, open Packs and tap "Restore purchases", or tap "Restore purchases" on the "Your whole History" sheet, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. The Android version has no purchases.

How do I cancel the History subscription?
Apple manages subscriptions, so cancel in the Settings app: tap your name → Subscriptions → Hapax → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment details.

I want my data deleted.
Hapax has no account and stores your history only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it, and we hold no copy anywhere else. Your purchases stay with your Apple Account, so you can restore them if you reinstall.

About

Hapax is made by Quietspire LLC, an independent app studio based in New Jersey, USA. A "hapax" is a word that appears exactly once in a text: which is how often you'll meet each word here: once a day, at midnight.
