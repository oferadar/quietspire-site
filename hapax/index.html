Four levels, from everyday words to ones even word lovers have never met. You pick the one that is hard for you, and your words come from it. Press one to see a word from it.

One new word a day on your iPhone Lock Screen and Home Screen, with what it means, how to say it, and an example.

440 entries, each written and checked by hand. The word of the day is free, there are no ads and no account, and the whole app works offline.

App Store Google Play

In the app

Widgets
The word on your Lock Screen and Home Screen. It changes at midnight, your local time, without opening the app.
On Android
A Home Screen widget, and, if you turn it on, today’s word as your lock screen wallpaper. Your home wallpaper is never changed.
Hear it
Tap the pronunciation to hear the word in your phone’s own voice. No internet needed.
Practice
Go back over the words you have already met. No streaks, no scores.
History
Every word you have met. On iPhone the last three days are free, and the whole History is a subscription or comes with The Whole Library.
Word packs
Extra words for tests like the SAT, GRE, TOEFL and IELTS, each bought once on the App Store.
Themes
Ten of them. This page wears two: Rose up here, and Light below.
Privacy
No ads, no account, no notifications. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”.
