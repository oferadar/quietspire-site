Beautiful words

serendipity

sair-uhn-DIP-ih-tee, noun

The occurrence of happy or beneficial events by chance.

“It was pure serendipity that the two founders shared a delayed flight.”

wistful

WIST-fuhl, adjective

Full of gentle, thoughtful longing.

“He went wistful at the smell of his grandmother’s spice cabinet.”

diaphanous

dahy-AF-uh-nuhs, adjective

Light, delicate, and translucent.

“Diaphanous curtains turned the noon sun to milk.”

ephemeral

ih-FEM-er-uhl, adjective

Lasting for a very short time.

“Internet fame is ephemeral; last month’s sensation is already forgotten.”

evanescent

ev-uh-NES-uhnt, adjective

Quickly fading from sight or memory.

“Fame online is evanescent; screenshots are forever.”

halcyon

HAL-see-uhn, adjective

Blissfully calm and prosperous, especially of a remembered past.

“He talks of the halcyon summers before the highway came.”
