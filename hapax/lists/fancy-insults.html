Fancy insults

Insults with better manners. Each one describes a real kind of annoying person, precisely enough that they may need a dictionary to take offense.

callow

KAL-oh, adjective

Inexperienced and immature.

“A callow reporter asked the question everyone else avoided.”

mendacious

men-DAY-shuhs, adjective

Habitually lying.

“The memoir was entertaining, mendacious, and a bestseller.”

obsequious

uhb-SEE-kwee-uhs, adjective

Excessively eager to please or obey.

“The obsequious waiter refilled glasses no one had touched.”

sycophant

SIK-uh-fuhnt, noun

A person who flatters the powerful to gain advantage.

“The director surrounded himself with sycophants who applauded every idea.”

TRUK-yuh-luhnt, adjective

Eager to argue or fight; aggressively defiant.

“The witness grew truculent under cross-examination.”

Excessively smooth and flattering; oily.
