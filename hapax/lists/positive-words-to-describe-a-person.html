Positive words to describe a person

Words for the people you admire, more precise than nice or smart: the friend who is easy to talk to, the colleague who never gives up, the reader who notices everything.

adept

uh-DEPT, adjective

Very skilled or talented at something.

“She proved adept at calming angry customers within her first week.”

earnest

UR-nist, adjective

Serious and sincere in intention.

“The new intern was so earnest that even the cynics rooted for him.”

humble

HUM-buhl, adjective

Modest about one’s own importance.

“Despite the awards, she stayed humble and kept teaching beginners.”

modest

MOD-ist, adjective

Unassuming about one’s own merits; moderate in size.

“He gave a modest shrug and credited the whole team.”

resilient

rih-ZIL-yuhnt, adjective

Able to recover quickly from setbacks or difficult conditions.

“The team proved resilient, rebounding from the failed launch within a quarter.”

savvy

SAV-ee, adjective

Having practical knowledge and sound judgment.

“A savvy shopper waits for the January sales.”
