Privacy Policy

Applies to the app Hapax. Last updated November 23, 2026.

About Hapax Support Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quietspire LLC ("Quietspire", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our vocabulary app Hapax (the "App").

The short version

Hapax collects no data. If you buy something in the App, Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your payment details.

What we don't collect

The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no servers of our own. It never asks for your name, email, location, or anything else, and it makes no network requests to us: there is nowhere to send data, because nothing is collected. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected", and Google Play’s data safety section says no data is collected or shared, because that is literally true.

Data on your device

Your word history, saved words, Practice progress, settings, and a record of what you have bought are stored on your device. None of it is sent to us. The optional tap-to-hear pronunciation uses your device's built-in speech engine, offline. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

Purchases

Hapax offers optional purchases, such as word packs and a subscription to the whole History. They are made through Apple's App Store, or Google Play on Android, and charged to your Apple Account or Google account. Apple or Google processes the payment. We never receive your card or other payment details.

To unlock what you bought, the App asks the store from your device which purchases your account owns, and keeps a copy of the answer on your device. Like every developer, we receive sales reports from Apple and Google; they do not include your payment details. Apple and Google handle purchase information under their own privacy policies. To restore a purchase or cancel a subscription, see Support.

Children's privacy

Hapax is suitable for a general audience. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA give you rights over personal data an app holds about you. Hapax holds none. There is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete: your data lives on your device, under your control, and removing the App removes it entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a new feature needs to handle data, the change will be described here before it takes effect, and the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
