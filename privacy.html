About SLAB Support Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quietspire LLC ("Quietspire", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our mobile game SLAB (the "Game"). By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version

SLAB has no login and does not ask you for your name, email, or other personal details. We don't run our own servers that store your data. The Game does use third-party tools from Unity, for showing ads and for gameplay analytics, and those tools automatically collect device and usage information as described below. That information is tied to identifiers for your device and your copy of the Game, so it is not anonymous.

Information collected automatically

Through the third-party services listed below, the following may be collected:

  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, language and region settings, processor, memory, screen size, battery level, sound volume, app version, and app performance and crash data.
  • Identifiers: a random ID that Unity Analytics creates for your installation of the Game; your device's identifier for vendor (IDFV); your advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking; and similar device identifiers the ad networks use to deliver and measure ads.
  • Approximate location: a coarse, country/region-level location derived from your IP address (we do not collect GPS/precise location).
  • Gameplay analytics: events sent with the identifiers above, such as each game you start and finish, your score and best score, perfect placements, crystals earned and spent, and daily gifts claimed. We use them to understand and improve the Game.
  • Purchase records: if you buy Remove Ads, Unity's purchasing tools add a record of it to the same analytics: the product, its price and currency, the transaction ID, and Apple's receipt for it. The receipt does not contain your payment details.
  • Ad data: the ads you are shown and how you interact with them, collected by the ad networks.

Your progress, high score, crystals, and settings are saved locally on your device. Your scores and crystal amounts are also included in the analytics events above.

Third-party services

SLAB uses the following third-party services, all provided by Unity Technologies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): the advertising mediation platform that serves optional rewarded ads, interstitial ads between some games, and banner ads on the menu screens. LevelPlay may draw on one or more ad networks (currently including Unity Ads) that use device identifiers to select, deliver, and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Analytics & Remote Config): for the gameplay analytics described above, and to adjust the Game's settings, such as difficulty and rewards, without an update.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. See Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Purchases and Game Center

SLAB sells one optional purchase, Remove Ads, through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your card or other payment details.

If you are signed in to Game Center, SLAB sends your best score to Apple's Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy. If you are not signed in, no score is sent.

Advertising & tracking choices

On iOS, if the Game or its ad partners wish to use your advertising identifier for tracking, you'll be asked for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can decline. You can also limit ad tracking at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising). Declining does not stop ads; it makes them less personalized.

If your device's region is set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, SLAB tells the ad platform not to show you personalized ads.

How the information is used

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To show and measure advertisements.
  • To understand how the Game is played, through analytics.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Children's privacy

SLAB is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing. Because we don't maintain accounts, the most effective control is your device's ad-tracking settings; you may also contact us to make a request.

Data retention

We don't run servers of our own. Unity stores the analytics data on our behalf, where we can view it, and keeps it according to Unity's retention policy. Data handled by the ad networks is retained according to their policies. Game data saved on your device remains there until you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
